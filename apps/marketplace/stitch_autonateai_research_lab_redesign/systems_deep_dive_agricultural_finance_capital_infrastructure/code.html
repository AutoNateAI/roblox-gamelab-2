PILLAR 01

Farm Credit System vs Commercial Banks

Government-Sponsored Enterprise Equity Arbitrage

The Farm Credit System operates as a borrower-owned cooperative GSE issuing consolidated system-wide debt via the Federal Farm Credit Banks Funding Corporation. Unlike depository commercial banks constrained by Federal Reserve reserve requirements and localized deposit bases, FCS entities maintain structural cost-of-funds advantages while returning patronage dividends directly to borrowers.

FCS Effective Spread Advantage 45 to 80 bps
Capital Structure Requirement Tier 1 Capital > 7.0%

Enterprise Banking & LOS Technology

nCino LOS, Salesforce CRM, and Legacy DB2 Cores

Agricultural lending institutions utilize a bifurcated IT stack. Modern loan origination and client relationship modules operate on Salesforce-backed nCino frameworks, while the underlying transaction processing and real-time interest compounding rely on hardened IBM DB2 or FIS Profile mainframes running high-integrity transaction queues.

PILLAR 03

Crop Insurance Risk Integration

USDA RMA Yield Protection & Assigned Indemnity Liens

Unsecured agricultural production lending does not exist in scale. Crop insurance (Revenue Protection – RP) policies serve as primary non-physical chattel security. Approved Operating notes mandate a legally perfected Assignment of Indemnity, transferring claim payout rights directly to the lender prior to residual farmer distribution.

Statutory Collateral Document Assignment of Indemnity (RMA-279)
Baseline Floor Coverage 75% - 85% Revenue Protection
Liquidity Realization Date Within 30 Days of Harvest Claim

-- AutoNateAI Core Credit Ledger: Production Operating Tranche Topology
CREATE TABLE agricultural_operating_tranche (
    tranche_uuid               UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    borrower_master_id         UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES borrower_entity(entity_uuid),
    fsa_farm_number            VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL,
    fsa_tract_number           VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL,
    crop_year                  SMALLINT NOT NULL CHECK (crop_year >= 2024),
    
    -- Credit Commitment & Pricing Spreads
    committed_amount_usd       NUMERIC(14, 2) NOT NULL,
    outstanding_draws_usd      NUMERIC(14, 2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    benchmark_index            VARCHAR(16) DEFAULT 'SOFR_30D_AVG',
    margin_spread_bps          INTEGER NOT NULL, -- Basis points over SOFR
    
    -- Security Perfection & Statutory Encumbrances
    ucc1_filing_number         VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    ucc1_jurisdiction_fips     VARCHAR(5) NOT NULL, -- e.g., '29201' for Scott County, MO
    rma_policy_number          VARCHAR(48) NOT NULL,
    indemnity_assignment_flag  BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    
    -- Grain Settlement Liquidation Directives
    authorized_terminal_id     VARCHAR(32) REFERENCES grain_terminal_registry(terminal_id),
    split_percentage_lender    NUMERIC(5, 2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 85.00, -- 85% diverted to debt service
    split_percentage_producer  NUMERIC(5, 2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 15.00, -- 15% residual to working capital
    
    -- Audit and Lifecycle Timestamps
    matures_at                 DATE NOT NULL,
    created_at                 TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    updated_at                 TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,

    CONSTRAINT unq_borrower_tract_year UNIQUE (borrower_master_id, fsa_tract_number, crop_year)
);
