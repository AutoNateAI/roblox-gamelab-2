## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor of an elite computational biology laboratory fused with continental agricultural logistics. It balances high-throughput environmental telemetry with peer-reviewed publication clarity.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Target Audience:** Agronomists, environmental systems researchers, freight supply chain directors, and automated ecological field engineers.
- **Tone:** Academic, authoritative, luminous, and analytically precise.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires quiet scientific confidence, absolute structural order, and clarity under high-density environmental monitoring.

### Design Movement: Technical Editorial & Luminous Precision
The design merges modern utilitarian data infrastructure with high-grade scientific journal layouts. It avoids heavy skeletal drop shadows and dark visual noise, utilizing ultra-clean ivory papers, low-contrast structural hairline boundaries, precise tabular alignment, and vibrant ecological accents.

## Colors

The palette simulates sunlight striking biological matter and archival publication paper, anchored by heavy analytical dark neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Ecological Core - `#059669` / `#10b981`):** Represents biological vital signs, sensor array nominal states, crop canopy indices, and verified field actions.
- **Secondary (Riverways & Freight - `#0284c7` / `#0369a1`):** Applied to telemetry routing, irrigation flows, barge corridors, and supply chain telemetry.
- **Tertiary (Harvest & Warning - `#d97706` / `#b45309`):** Reserved for ecological threshold alerts, soil moisture deficits, thermal stressors, and critical overrides.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#fbfcfb` (luminous ivory paper).
  - Raised cards & analytical panels: `#ffffff` (sterile clean paper).
  - Recessed wells & table track headers: `#f4f6f4` (soft biological ground).
- **Hairlines & Boundaries:** Use `#e2e8f0` tinted with subtle emerald `#d1fae5` to preserve distinct panel separations without visual weight.
- **Text & Readings:** `#111827` for dominant empirical values, `#4b5563` for scientific metadata and units.

## Typography

Typography functions as an analytical instrument. Proportions align to classic scientific journals while supporting real-time streaming telemetry.

- **Primary Matrix (Inter):** Deployed across all narrative hierarchies, panel titles, documentation, and interface prose. Tight letter-spacing ensures a structured, technical posture.
- **Telemetry & Readout Matrix (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for sensor inputs, coordinates (lat/long), chemical ratios, flow rates, timestamps, and programmatic indices. All tabular data arrays must utilize tabular lining figures.
- **Labeling Standard:** All data labels, micro-headers, and status indicators are rendered using `label-caps` in uppercase with expanded tracking for immediate identification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column dynamic fluid grid system engineered for analytical density without visual compression.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margin. High-density sidebars, real-time map views, and synchronized telemetry tables sit side-by-side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) margin. Collapses secondary telemetry streams into horizontal sliding modules.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margin. Telemetry tables convert into prioritized card lists with expandable details.

### Spacing Principles
- Density must reflect lab instrumentation: internal component gaps stay tight (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while structural module separations utilize `space-lg` to `space-xl` to avoid cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through luminous surface layering and hairline structural boundaries rather than deep diffuse drops.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Canvas (Base Ground - `#fbfcfb`):** The primary environmental bed.
2. **Container (Panel - `#ffffff`):** Framed with a 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`). 
3. **Floating Overlays & Dropdowns:** Pure `#ffffff` surface with a specialized botanical ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
4. **Data Insets & Recessed Cells:** Background `#f4f6f4` with an inner hairline border (`#e5e7eb`) creating an engraved lab-plate appearance.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses level 1 soft roundedness (`0.25rem` / `4px`), prioritizing sharp data visualization boundaries, crisp telemetry borders, and paper card edges reminiscent of lab specimen slides and scientific print media.

- Small controls, inputs, and chips: `rounded` (`0.25rem`).
- Cards, panels, and data grids: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem`).
- Badges and pill indicators: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to immediately contrast against square data blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Field Action):** Solid `#059669` fill, white text, subtle hover shift to `#047857`, 1px border of `#047857`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#10b981`.
- **Secondary (Logistics / Routing):** Solid `#0284c7` fill, white text, hover shift to `#0369a1`.
- **Outline / Lab Tool:** High-contrast `#ffffff` paper surface, `#111827` typography, 1px `#e2e8f0` border, hover background `#f4f6f4`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Biological Nominal:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `#a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`, font `label-caps`.
- **Freight / Hydrological Active:** Background `#f0f9ff`, border `#bae6fd`, text `#075985`, font `label-caps`.
- **Threshold Caution:** Background `#fffbeb`, border `#fde68a`, text `#92400e`, font `label-caps`.

### Telemetry Cards
Pure white `#ffffff` paper surfaces wrapped in 1px `#e2e8f0` borders. Top border features a 2px colored accent thread (Emerald for ecology, River Blue for logistics, Amber for thresholds). Metrics display large Inter digits accompanied by small JetBrains Mono units.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Header row: `#f4f6f4` background, uppercase `label-caps` typography, 1px bottom border `#cbd5e1`.
- Data rows: `#ffffff` alternating with crisp `#fafbfa`, row hover `#f0fdf4`.
- Numeric data columns must align right and utilize `data-mono-md`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#ffffff` field, 1px `#d1d5db` border, internal label in `label-caps`. On focus: border becomes `#059669` with a 2px `#d1fae5` glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Compact 16px geometric squares/circles. Active state is solid `#059669` with crisp white SVG indicators.

### Specialized Lab Telemetry Strip
Persistent horizontal ribbon displaying system heartbeat, sensor latency, and aggregate field indices using `data-mono-md` across `#f4f6f4` with emerald status pulse beacons.
