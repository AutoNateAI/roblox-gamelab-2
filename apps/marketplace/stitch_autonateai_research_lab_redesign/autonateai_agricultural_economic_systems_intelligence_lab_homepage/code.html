Spatial Modeling of Unit Train Dwell Delays Across Cairo Interchange Junctions

An empirical audit assessing how class-I rail switching congestion creates an artificial $0.12 basis depression at regional elevators within a 45-mile radius.

SQL SCHEMA // INTERCHANGE_CORRIDOR_V2
SELECT 
    elevator_id, 
    AVG(basis_spread_cents) AS avg_basis_drag,
    dwell_hours_cairo
FROM semo_grain_terminals
WHERE rail_carrier = 'UP_BNSF_EXCHANGE'
GROUP BY elevator_id, dwell_hours_cairo
HAVING dwell_hours_cairo > 36.0;
