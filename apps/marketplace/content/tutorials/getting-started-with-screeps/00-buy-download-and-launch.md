# Buy, Download, and Launch Screeps on Steam

## 1. Buy Screeps: World

Open Steam and search for **Screeps: World**. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription — buying it unlocks the full client and your first CPU allotment. Click **Add to Cart**, check out, and let it drop into your Steam library.

## 2. Download and Install

From your Steam Library, select Screeps: World and click **Install**. Pick an install location if prompted and wait for the download to finish. No extra launcher or account setup is required at this step — Steam handles the client.

## 3. Launch and Sign In

Click **Play**. On first launch you'll be asked to sign in or create a Screeps account (email + password, or a linked Steam identity depending on the build). Accept the terms and let the client finish loading assets — this can take a minute the first time.

## 4. Reach the World Map

After sign-in, you should land on the **world map** — a grid of sectors and rooms, some owned, most empty. If you see the map and can pan/zoom around it, you're ready. This is the screen the next tutorial picks up from.

## 5. Sanity Checks

If Steam says it's installed but nothing launches: right-click Screeps: World in your library → **Properties** → **Installed Files** → **Verify integrity of game files**.

If the client hangs on a loading screen: check your connection, then fully quit and relaunch — the first asset load is the slowest one.

If you don't see the world map after signing in: confirm the account finished creating (check your email for a verification link) and sign in again.
