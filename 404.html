route How a Series Gets Made

From a question to a season.

  1. 01travel_explore

    Research

    We investigate the subject and your audience: curriculum, field research, sources, interviews, the real names and places your people know.

  2. 02edit_note

    Story

    We build the spine: a human story where the concept is the way out, a diverse cast with backstories, an arc that pays off across episodes.

  3. 03movie

    Production

    Script, voice, music and animation. Lessons become full-screen visual explanations; drama and comedy carry the viewer between them.

  4. 04rate_review

    Review

    You watch every cut on a private review link and sign off before anything goes public. Edits re-render scene by scene.

  5. 05rocket_launch

    Deliver

    Published to your channel or ours: playlists, SEO descriptions with chapters, thumbnails, reels, PDFs, a landing page.

help FAQ

Questions people ask.

What does AutoNateAI actually make?expand_more

Animated series built on real research. We investigate a subject, build a story and a cast around it, and produce finished episodes with the teaching visuals, reels and learning materials that go with them.

How is AutoNateAI related to Bot That Lyfe?expand_more

Bot That Lyfe (botthatlyfe.com) is where we publish our original computer-science series and free courses. AutoNateAI is the research and commercial side: independent investigations, commissioned series and partnerships. Bot That Lyfe is the proof of what the studio makes.

Do you use AI?expand_more

Yes, behind the scenes: research tooling, voice, and a production engine we built ourselves. The stories, the research checks and the final cut are human decisions, and every series is reviewed before it's published.

How long does a series take?expand_more

A single explainer can turn around in days. A six-episode series typically takes a few weeks from research to final delivery, with review links along the way.
