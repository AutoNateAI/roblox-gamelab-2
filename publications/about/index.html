Virtual Cell Lab is an independent research project by Nathan Baker, a software engineer moving into computational biology in public.

The work starts from experiments other scientists have already run. We hide the answers from a model, ask it to predict them, and check. The videos are hosted by Cellie, an animated cell. The posts here carry the full scripts, sources and findings, including the results that didn't work.

Every claim is labelled by what kind of claim it is: background science, a published finding, a model's output, or our own hypothesis. A model's prediction is not a biological result, and nothing here is medical advice.

This project is not affiliated with, reviewed by or endorsed by Arc Institute or any other lab or institute named in it, and its narration is AI-generated.
