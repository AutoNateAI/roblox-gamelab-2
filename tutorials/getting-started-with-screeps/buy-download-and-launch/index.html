Into the Arena: Buy, Download, and Launch Screeps on Steam

AutoNate finally buys the game he's been training for. Purchase Screeps: World on Steam, install the client, sign in, and land on the world map ready to claim your first room.

Buy on SteamInstall & launchReach the world map

If you've been riding with AutoNate since he wrote his first console.log, welcome back — this is the day everything he learned stops being practice. If this is the first you're meeting him: he's eighteen, he taught himself JavaScript from nothing over the last few weeks, and he's about to buy the actual game he's been training for. Either way, you don't need any of that backstory to follow this guide — every step here works whether you've been coding for five minutes or five years. If you do want the fuller story of how he got here, that's the Intro to JavaScript for Beginners pack, and it's free too.

AutoNate had the Steam page open in a tab for two full days before he actually clicked buy. Not because the price was bad — because clicking it made the whole thing real. Up until now, "I'm gonna learn to code and build a bot" was just a thing he said. Once he pays for the game, it's a thing he's doing. He checked his bank app one more time like the number was going to change, took a breath, and hit the button. You might do the same thing. That's normal. Buy it anyway.

1. Buy Screeps: World

Open Steam and search for Screeps: World. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription — buying it unlocks the full client and your first CPU allotment. Click Add to Cart, check out, and let it drop into your Steam library.

2. Download and Install

From your Steam Library, select Screeps: World and click Install. Pick an install location if prompted and wait for the download to finish. No extra launcher or account setup is required at this step — Steam handles the client.

This is the part where AutoNate paced his room for ten minutes watching a progress bar like it owed him money. A download bar has never moved slower for anyone, ever, in the history of the internet. Go make a sandwich.

3. Launch and Sign In

Click Play. On first launch you'll be asked to sign in or create a Screeps account (email + password, or a linked Steam identity depending on the build). Accept the terms and let the client finish loading assets — this can take a minute the first time.

4. Reach the World Map

After sign-in, you should land on the world map — a grid of sectors and rooms, some owned, most empty. If you see the map and can pan/zoom around it, you're ready. This is the screen the next tutorial picks up from.

(Placeholder screenshot from the official Screeps site — swap in your own once you've claimed a room.)

AutoNate sat there for a solid minute just scrolling the map, zooming in and out, not doing anything productive at all. Every one of those little rooms is somebody's actual colony — some abandoned, some running quietly, some being fought over right now by two people who will never meet in real life. It's a whole world, and none of it needed him to press a single button to keep going while he was staring at it. That part hit different. This isn't a level that waits for you to load in. It's already running.

5. Sanity Checks

If Steam says it's installed but nothing launches: right-click Screeps: World in your library → Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files.

If the client hangs on a loading screen: check your connection, then fully quit and relaunch — the first asset load is the slowest one.

If you don't see the world map after signing in: confirm the account finished creating (check your email for a verification link) and sign in again.

AutoNate's got the map open. Somewhere in this grid is a room with his name about to go on it. Time to go claim it.
