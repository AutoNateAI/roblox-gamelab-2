auto_stories Mission

Make hard things understandable, and worth watching.

The most important ideas are often the hardest to explain: proofs, data, money, health, how a program or a community actually works. We believe the best way to teach them is the oldest one: a good story, with characters people care about and a lesson they remember. The research makes it true. The story makes it stick.

diamond Standards

How we work.

Research first

We don't write a scene we haven't understood. Every series starts with an investigation and ships with its sources.

Picture first

We teach the way people learn: the picture, then the numbers, then the words, then the symbols.

Integrity

Fiction is labeled as fiction, real people are named but never portrayed, and claims are checked.

Made to be used

A series comes with what teachers, programs and marketers need to put it to work.
