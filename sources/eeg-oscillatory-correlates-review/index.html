EEG Oscillatory Correlates of Meditation Practice: Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis

Multiple authors — Neuroscience / Elsevier

Read the Source open_in_new

Key Insight

Pooled alpha/beta/gamma effects during meditation are real, but measurement state materially changes effect sizes — 'one frequency = one mental state' is not supported.

What's Actually Supported

Pooled meditation-related increases in alpha, beta, and gamma power are consistently reported across the reviewed literature.

Caveat / Limitations

Heterogeneity remains high across practice types, outcomes, and feedback targets — frequency-band findings aren't universal biomarkers of mindfulness.
