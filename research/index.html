The Curve Discrete mathematics & proofs (EECS 203)

Mapped the EECS 203 syllabus (logic, proofs, sets & functions, induction, counting, graphs) to six episodes; 18 proofs worked and checked; every problem in the style of the course text.

Sources

  • Rosen, Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications
  • Wason (1968), the selection task
  • Cantor (1891), the diagonal argument
  • Euler (1736), the bridges of Königsberg
  • Golomb (1954), tromino tilings
